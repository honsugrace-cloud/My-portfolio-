GET TO KNOW ME

About The Artist

Hello! My name is John Kingsley. I am a Form Three Visual Arts student with a passion for pencil drawing, portraiture and creative expression.

Art is more than drawing to me. It is a way of expressing ideas, capturing emotions and bringing imagination to life. I am constantly learning, practising and developing my artistic skills.

My dream is to grow as an artist, share my creativity with the world and create beautiful artworks for people who appreciate art.
